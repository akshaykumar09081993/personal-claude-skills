Privacy Policy

Sparklinx Automation · Last updated: 2026-10-15

Sparklinx Automation ("the app") is a private, single-user automation operated by its owner for their own Google account (sparklinxgroup@gmail.com). This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail: the app searches the owner's mailbox for one specific daily sales report email, forwards that email to a designated recipient, and applies a label so the message is not processed twice.
  • Google Drive: the app saves the report's spreadsheet attachment into a folder it creates in the owner's own Drive. It uses the drive.file scope, so it can only see files it created.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to forward and archive the owner's own daily report. The app runs on a schedule in the owner's own Google Cloud project. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond the forwarding and archiving described above.

Data retention & storage

Forwarded emails remain in the owner's Gmail; archived attachments remain in the owner's Google Drive. The app stores no separate copy of message contents. OAuth credentials are stored securely in the owner's Google Cloud Secret Manager.

Access revocation

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
